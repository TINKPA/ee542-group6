    Prototype notes for the team

    All data is sample data. The embedding model and SLM are mocked in the page: search uses a small hashed word-vector embedding with cosine similarity, and the agent's questions and replies come from rules. Every back-end call goes through LegacyAPI; set CONFIG.apiBase at the top of the script to send them to the team's server instead (endpoints are listed in the requirements doc, §7). The published artifact cannot reach outside servers, so test the live mode from a local copy.
