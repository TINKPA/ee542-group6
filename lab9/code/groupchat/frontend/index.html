F-1 Compass

A group chat for USC international students, with a bot that answers F-1, OPT, CPT and travel questions from official sources (8 CFR 214.2(f), USCIS, SEVP, ICE, USC OIS) and links each answer to them.

Create an account or log in to join the chat.

Messages that contain a question mark “?” are answered by the F-1 Compass bot. It gives general information, not legal advice: confirm decisions with your DSO at USC OIS.
